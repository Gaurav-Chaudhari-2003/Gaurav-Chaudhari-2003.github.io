Background & Philosophy

I'm Gaurav Vilas Chaudhari, an AI Engineer at Stark Digital, Pune. I build retrieval pipelines, natural-language analytics engines, vision telemetry and edge-AI systems for enterprise and public-sector clients.

I got here through the whole stack: a Diploma in Computer Engineering at Government Polytechnic, Achalpur, then a B.E. in Computer Science & Engineering at SSGMCE, Shegaon. Models are the easy part. I engineer what surrounds them (retrieval, orchestration, queues and telemetry) so they hold up in production.

SYS_01Natural language → SQLPMRDA · PMC

Natural-Language to SQL Analytics Engine

An enterprise text-to-SQL assistant for the Pune Metropolitan Region Development Authority and Pune Municipal Corporation RTS databases. It resolves who is asking, filters the live schema to what they may see, generates safe PostgreSQL and streams back tables, charts and an executive summary.

  • Live PostgreSQL 16 schema discovery with semantic filtering (bak_* snapshots excluded)
  • User-aware RBAC and row-level security on every generated query
  • SSE streaming of SQL, recordset, Plotly chart and summary
  • Questions in English, Marathi and Hinglish
  • FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL 16
  • Vanna 2.0
  • OpenRouter
  • Plotly
